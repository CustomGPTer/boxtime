No adverts in BoxTime, adverts in the others

BoxTime has no paid version, no subscription and no adverts. The best-known boxing timers on Google Play are free to install and pay their way with adverts.

Download for Android

Free. Android 10 or later. What it does

Where your training is kept

BoxTime keeps your timers and history on your phone and runs a session with or without an internet connection. It has no account and no servers. If that matters to you, check each app's privacy policy and its Data safety section on Google Play before you install it.

What BoxTime does not do

  • It is not on Google Play, so it is installed from a downloaded file and updated by installing the new version over the old one.
  • It has no shake or wave-to-start sensor. Brucemax's listing describes one for starting a round without taking your gloves off. BoxTime's answer is tap anywhere to pause and a tap-lock.
  • The voice is your phone's built-in voice, not a recorded announcer, so it sounds the way your phone sounds. If the phone has no voice installed, the bells still play and the voice stays silent.
  • The watch app is a separate file and has to be put on the watch over Wi-Fi with developer options, because it is not on Google Play.
  • The TV clock works through screen mirroring or a cable. It does not cast straight to a Chromecast.
  • It does not measure heart rate, count punches or estimate calories.
  • The app is in English only.
