Release notes

What changed in each version of BoxTime. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.0 16 November 2026Latest

  • One set-up screen for preparation, round, rest and the number of rounds, with number wheels and ready times to tap.
  • Fifteen fight presets: pro and amateur boxing, title fight, novice, white collar, MMA, Muay Thai, kickboxing, bag work, pad work, sparring, skipping, Tabata and a five-round bag ladder.
  • Four timer faces: Boxed, Plain, Ring and a sideways Gym face with the largest digits.
  • Twenty-one built-in sounds, all made inside the app, and your own sound files.
  • Separate sounds for the countdown, the start of a round, the end of a round and the end of the session.
  • A warning before the end of a round and before the end of a rest, each with its own sound and its own number of seconds.
  • Spoken cues in the phone's own voice: round number, final round, rest, halfway, seconds left and session complete, each with its own switch.
  • Combo callouts: punches 1 to 6 with body shots and defence, three levels, three paces and your own combinations. Off until switched on.
  • Round plans: a name for each round, and a round or rest length of its own for any round. Off until switched on.
  • Warm-up and cool-down blocks.
  • Sessions carry on with the screen off, with the countdown and Pause, Skip and Finish on the lock screen. A switch keeps the screen on instead.
  • Music dips for bells and voice. It can pause instead, or be left alone.
  • Vibration and a screen flash on the bells.
  • Tap anywhere to pause, and a tap-lock button. Both off until switched on.
  • Unlimited saved timers with names, favourites, rename and duplicate.
  • History: a session log, total rounds and time in rounds, day and week streaks and a month calendar.
  • Sharing a timer by QR code or as a file, and scanning or opening one sent to you.
  • A home-screen widget that starts your favourite timer.
  • A full-screen clock on a TV or monitor when the phone is mirrored or connected by cable.
  • A companion app for Wear OS watches with the countdown, a buzz on the bells and back, pause and skip buttons.
  • Six colour themes, and any colour for each part of the session.
  • Backup to a file and restore from one.
  • A notice when a newer version is out.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.0 is called BoxTime.apk. It is 2.8 MB (2,776,921 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

2b5ce76a72337644c23c1fe0e207f35c7d541a20d8bfc370e57350ca3f8512ca

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile BoxTime.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
